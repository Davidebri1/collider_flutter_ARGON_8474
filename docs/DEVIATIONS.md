# Deviations list

Made by Claude on 2026-10-04, because David asked for it:
"i dont want any approximations. i want relationships. the %'s should carry over. unless you think otherwise,
then write it on your deviations list and provide justification. do that for each deviation."

How a frame is carried: every space, size and radius of the frame is multiplied by one scale for that screen,
so the relationships hold. Each place where the app does not do that is listed here with its reason.
The frames are the ones in David's map of 4 Oct 2026 (docs\FIGMA_REFERENCE_MAP_1004.md).
